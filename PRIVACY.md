# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2026**

Your privacy is important to us. This extension processes data locally on your device whenever possible.

## Data Collection
We do not collect personal data or chat history. All processing happens within your browser environment.

## Permissions
This extension requires access to the active tab to extract chat content. This permission is used solely for the purpose of exporting your selected messages.

## Contact
If you have questions, please use the contact form in the menu.
