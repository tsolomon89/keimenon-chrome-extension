# Keimenon Lite Product Description

## Summary
Extract and export your prompts or full conversations from AI chat pages (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) locally. Privacy-first, no servers.

## Description
**Keimenon Lite** is the ultimate privacy-first tool for power users of AI chat interfaces. It allows you to instantly extract, filter, and archive your *own* prompt history—or entire conversations—without capturing AI responses or sending a single byte of data to a third-party server.

Whether you are building a curated prompt library, auditing your interactions for compliance, or simply saving a conversation for personal notes, Keimenon Lite gives you full control over your data with a premium, Material Design 3 interface that sits comfortably in your browser's side panel.

### Why Install Keimenon Lite?

**1. Uncompromising Privacy (Local-Only)**
In an era where data privacy is paramount, Keimenon Lite stands apart.
*   **No Servers:** We do not have a backend. Your chat data never leaves your machine.
*   **Transient Memory:** Data is extracted in real-time and held in RAM only while the extension is open. Once you close it, the data is gone—leaving no trace.

**2. Powerful Extraction & Radar Scanning**
Stop manually copying and pasting.
*   **Smart Radar:** Automatically detects and extracts messages from infinite-scroll chats (like long Claude sessions) by scrolling back through history for you.
*   **User-Only Mode:** specifically target *your* input to build a clean repository of your own prompts, separating your creative input from the AI's output.

**3. Multi-Platform Support**
One tool for all your AI workflows. Seamlessly works on:
*   **ChatGPT** (`chatgpt.com`, `chat.openai.com`)
*   **Claude** (`claude.ai`)
*   **Gemini** (`gemini.google.com`)
*   **Grok** (`x.com`, `grok.com`)

**4. Advanced Search & Organization**
Find exactly what you wrote, when you wrote it.
*   **Instant Search:** Filter through hundreds of prompts in milliseconds with real-time highlighting.
*   **Smart Filtering:** Sort by "Longest First" to find your most complex prompts, or hide short "continue" messages to focus on significant interactions.

**5. One-Click Export**
*   **Copy:** Instantly copy a single message card or the entire filtered list.
*   **Clean Format:** Exports are formatted as clean markdown or plain text, ready to be pasted directly into Notion, Obsidian, or your favorite note-taking app.

### Key Features Summary
*   **Privacy:** 100% Client-side processing.
*   **Design:** Beautiful Material Design 3 (M3) UI.
*   **Efficiency:** "Radar" scan for deep history extraction.
*   **Flexibility:** Supports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.
